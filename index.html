Pedro Tavares

Hello there! I hope you are well and welcome to my personal website. I am a frontend developer based in the south east of England, and I have been working in the web and creative industries for over 20 years.

I started my career as a CGI generalist, working on short films, commercials and websites. Then I landed a job working with Flash creating web-banners for the entertainment industry. Once the Flash era ended, I moved to HTML5 and CSS3, and I have been working with these technologies ever since.

Projects as a frontend developer.

  1. Above Surveying

    Lead frontend developer. In charge of the company's main client application frontend code and reviewing and giving feedback on UI and UX decisions.

    Championed the implementation of component-based architecture for the UI elements, encouraged the use of Storybook for UI component development. Implemented and mentored others in the use of Playwright and Percy for E2E testing and the importance of visual regression testing.

  2. Postscript Medical

    Sole developer and infrastructure architect, responsible for the full technical delivery of an MVP trialled by two London NHS hospitals; the trial was well received, with some adoption of the tool among trial users.
