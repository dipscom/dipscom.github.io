Pedro Tavares

I am a professional with over 20 years of experience in the digital industry, 11 of those as a frontend developer. I have a strong background in HTML, CSS and JavaScript. I am passionate about creating beautiful and functional user interfaces, that are accessible and performant.

My career started as a CGI generalist, working on short films, commercials and web-based animations. Then I landed a job working with Flash creating web-banners for the entertainment industry. Once the Flash era ended, I moved to HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and I have been working with these technologies ever since.

Over the years I have worked in many different projects with many different tech setups and team sizes: from small static webisites to large web applications, interactive screens for trade shows, gamified experiences, mobile-first designs and 3D visualisations. The only constant in all these projects has been the need to adapt and learn. I am confident in my ability to absorb new information and work with it.

If you want to get in touch, you can reach me via my profile on LinkedIn.

Projects as a frontend developer.

  1. Above Surveying

    www.abovesurveying.com

    March 2022 – July 2026

    Led the frontend development of a web application that allows companies to manage and track the status of their solar farms.

    Implemented a design system and component library using Storybook, allowing for a more consistent and efficient development process. Introduced the use of Playwright for E2E testing and Percy for visual regression testing, ensuring that the application remains stable and visually consistent across updates.

    Led the migration of the frontend codebase from a legacy PHP and jQuery setup to a modern Svelte and TypeScript architecture, improving performance and maintainability and was responsible for setting up many of the UX and UI patterns and components.

    Envisaged and implemented a MVC-inspired architecture for the frontend codebase leveraging Svelte's reactive runes resulting in a more modular, scalable and maintainable codebase.

    Implemented several Leaflet.js maps with custom markers, inputs, overlays and popups of varying levels of complexity and data density.

    Took part in discusions and planning of the backend REST API endpoints, ensuring that the frontend and backend are well integrated and that the data is structured in a way that is easy to consume and manipulate on the frontend.
